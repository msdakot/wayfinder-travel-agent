When a delay breaks the next part of your trip, Wayfinder does not send you back to search. It compares the available routes against your arrival, hotel deadline, personal plans, and spending limit. Choose the recommended option and confirm once. Wayfinder marks the original connection as missed, adds the replacement train, updates your expected hotel arrival and reminders, and keeps the decision in the trip history. You stay in control, while the itinerary stays current.
