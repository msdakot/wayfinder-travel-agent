No more searching across emails, PDFs, airline apps, and maps. Wayfinder turns trip documents into one living itinerary. The Chile journey appears across flights, stays, activities, tickets, and mapped stops. It shows how to get there, keeps each source document beside the plan, and monitors the journey. When a delay appears, Wayfinder identifies the tighter connection and explains the downstream impact.

An alert should also help you move. In this fictional London recovery example, the original train is no longer reachable. Wayfinder compares alternatives against arrival time, hotel check-in, personal plans, and budget. The traveler chooses the recommended train and confirms once. The old connection is marked missed, the 9:15 replacement becomes active, hotel arrival and reminders update, and the decision stays in trip history. You approve the choice. Wayfinder keeps the journey current.
