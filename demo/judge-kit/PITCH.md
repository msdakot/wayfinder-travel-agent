# Wayfinder — three-minute pitch

## 0:00–0:22 — Hook

Travel breaks as a chain, but every company sees only one link. The airline sees a delayed flight. The rail operator sees a ticket. The hotel sees a check-in. The traveler must discover how one change affects everything else. Wayfinder turns those disconnected reservations into one living journey.

## 0:22–0:47 — Problem

Today, the traveler searches email, PDFs, provider apps, and maps. A delay creates three urgent questions: What changed? What breaks next? Which alternative works for my time, budget, and commitments? Existing tools report status; they rarely resolve that complete decision.

## 0:47–1:18 — Product

The traveler connects an inbox or selects up to fifteen documents. Wayfinder extracts flights, stays, transport, activities, and tickets into one timeline. Every item keeps its source. Locations become interactive routes. The traveler sees what matters now: where to go, when to leave, whether the trip is on time, and what needs attention.

## 1:18–1:48 — Proof

In our live demo, five documents create an eighteen-item Chile itinerary. A provider-style update reports a seventy-one-minute delay, and Wayfinder checks every downstream dependency. The Santiago arrival moves to 8:56 AM, leaving thirty-seven minutes beyond the reviewed transfer buffer, so the Calama connection is flagged. One question returns a concise, cited answer.

## 1:48–2:15 — Decision and action

When recovery is required, Wayfinder compares alternatives against arrival time, hotel check-in, personal plans, policies, and budget. It rejects options that do not work and asks for one approval. The missed connection remains visible, the replacement becomes active, reminders move, and the decision is recorded. Nothing is purchased, canceled, or charged without confirmation.

## 2:15–2:36 — Snowflake and Claude

Snowflake is the trusted context layer for reviewed itinerary facts, the current disruption state, and searchable policy evidence. Cortex Analyst answers structured questions. Cortex Search retrieves supporting passages. A Cortex Agent powered by Claude connects that evidence into a concise explanation. Deterministic code remains authoritative for timing, feasibility, cost, and approval state.

## 2:36–2:53 — Evaluation

We test four boundaries: extraction fidelity; impact correctness; grounded answers whose citations stay inside reviewed evidence; and action safety that rejects infeasible, stale, duplicate, or unapproved changes. Our golden check is five documents to eighteen items, then a seventy-one-minute delay to exactly one at-risk connection. Seventy automated tests cover the wider workflow.

## 2:53–3:00 — Close

Wayfinder removes the work between an alert and a safe decision. The traveler stays in control, while the whole journey stays current.
